The final project is your opportunity to bring together what you have learned across the six modules and apply it to a problem of your own choosing.

## What you will do

You will define and carry out your **own project** on a topic that relates to the content of the course — for example, working with a real biological dataset, applying machine learning or data-analysis methods, and using AI tools and agents to support your exploration, coding, and analysis.

You are expected to:

- Choose a scientific question and a relevant dataset that connect to the course material.
- Explore the data and apply appropriate methods and models.
- Use AI tools/agents responsibly, and document how you used them.
- Report your work and (for Master's students) present it orally.

## Working arrangements

You may work individually or in a small team, following the arrangements announced during the course. Each participant is responsible for demonstrating their own understanding and contributions.

## Format, deliverables, and deadlines

**The detailed project format — including specific deliverables, submission instructions, evaluation criteria, and deadlines — will be announced during the course and published on this page.**

## Assessment

**Master's students:**
- Final project report — pass/fail.
- Oral presentation (oral exam) — **mandatory**, graded **A–F**. This grade is your course grade.

**PhD students and other participants:**
- Final project report — pass/fail.
- Oral presentation — **optional** (you are welcome to present as time allows).
- The project is assessed on a **pass/fail** basis.

For questions about the final project, contact the course team at <ddls-course@scilifelab.se>.
