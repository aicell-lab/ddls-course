You have a dataset and a question you have never had time to answer, and a student on the
Data-Driven Life Sciences course (KTH / SciLifeLab) is going to take it on. They direct an AI agent
to do the analysis and check every result themselves; **you supply the problem and the judgement of
whether the answer is useful.** This page is everything that matters from your side.

---

## What we ask of you

**About two hours, total.** Two one-hour online meetings, roughly three weeks apart. Everything
between and after them happens without you.

- It is **free**, and there is no obligation — you can stop at any point.
- The results are **yours**. We make no claim on your data, your results, or authorship.
- At the end you get a **written report and a small analysis app or the code**, which is yours to keep.

---

## How it works

{{< project-workflow >}}

| Step | Your time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Matched** | none | We pair you with a student and confirm the project. You get a private link to your project page. |
| **2. Meeting 1** | **1 hour, online** | The student interviews you about the question — and **this is where you hand over the data** (see below). |
| **3. They build** | none | Two or three days. The student directs an AI agent to do the analysis and checks every result. |
| **4. Meeting 2** | **1 hour, online** | They show you what they built; **you tell them what is wrong**. Being corrected here is the point, not a failure. |
| **5. Optional rounds** | by agreement | Only if you both want to — never assumed. |
| **6. You get the results** | none | A short written report plus a runnable app or code. The student hands their work to the course for assessment, and **we share the results back to you**. |

---

## How your data is handled

Your data is yours, and we treat it that way. You choose how it is shared, at Meeting 1:

- **A copy or an extract.** A de-identified or subsampled extract is usually plenty — you do not have
  to hand over everything.
- **A protected environment (the default for anything sensitive).** We run a small, locked workspace
  on a course server (KTH SciLifeLab). You upload your data into it; the student's agent can **run
  analysis inside and see results, but cannot download or copy the raw data out.** Every command and
  output is checked. When the project ends, the workspace is torn down.
- **On your own machine.** If you would rather not move the data at all, the student can design the
  analysis and you run it yourself. That is a perfectly good choice.

We never move or upload data you have not explicitly agreed to move.

## How you follow along

You have your **own private project page** — a link we email you, with **no account or password**. It
is the single place the whole project lives, so nothing gets lost in email. There you can:

- **upload your data and a sample file** — the tidiest way to hand it over;
- **watch the stage move** — matched → meeting 1 → building → meeting 2 → delivered;
- **read the student's progress updates and reports**, including the final one;
- **raise a question or an issue** in a thread the student and the teaching team both see;
- **get the occasional email** on the big moments — which you can turn off.

Lost your link? Just email us and we will re-send it.
